<p> Emmanuel Bwiire, one of the Ugandan music duo Benti Boys, has been reported missing by his brother and fellow duo member, Steven, who made a public appeal for information during an interview with Baba TV urging anyone with details about Bwiire’s whereabouts to come forward.</p><p>According to Steven, Bwiire left their shared home in his car on Monday, June 9, 2025, at around 2:00 PM. Since then, his phone has been switched off and unreachable, including on WhatsApp, which Steven described as highly unusual.</p><p>“We always keep each other informed about our whereabouts,” Steven says during appearance with Baba T.V</p><p>The duo, known as Benti Boys Africa, gained significant recognition in Uganda’s vibrant music scene, especially after releasing their hit song “Wanawankya.”</p><p>The track, produced with the help of renowned producer Daddy Andre, became a chart-topping sensation and marked a breakthrough for the multi-talented brothers, who also gained attention with earlier releases like “Birungi” and “Nakuwona.”</p><p>Their dynamic performances and unique sound have made them a beloved act in the industry.</p><p>Bwiire was spotted at Temperature Touch Studios on the night before they headed to Pier One for a scheduled performance.</p><p>After the event, the brothers part ways, and Bwiire’s disappearance was noted when he failed to return home and  respond to attempts to contact him.</p><p> </p><p>Read Also: <a href="https://info.ssegu.com/what-is-eps-electronic-penalty-system-hunting-speed-drivers/">EPS System Hunting Speed Drivers (Electronic Penalty System).</a></p><p> </p><p> </p><div>Content originally published on SSEGU.
