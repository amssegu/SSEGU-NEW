<p> On Tuesday, June 24, 2025, Uganda’s gospel music community was heartbroken by the unexpected passing of the beloved singer Daudi Mugema.</p><p>Fans were devastated to learn that he was found unresponsive in his hotel room in Gulu.</p><p>Daudi is  known for his inspiring hit song “Katonda w’abanaku teyeebaka” and had touched the hearts of many with his devotion to gospel music with unique style of craft.</p><p>The circumstances surrounding his passing are still under investigation, as authorities work to ascertain the cause of his  death.</p><p>Daudi had traveled to Gulu in hopes of seeking financial assistance from General Salim Saleh, a notable military figure, to help him with ongoing health challenges that had led him to seek specialized treatment abroad.</p><p>Last year, he received support from Kampala’s Lord Mayor, Erias Lukwago, which enabled him to travel to Turkey for care related to a leg condition.</p>[caption id="attachment_6529" align="aligncenter" width="240"]<em>Courtesy photo Late Daudi Mugema and Lord Mayor Erias Lukwago with Turkish Ambassador To Uganda</em>[/caption]<p>Upon his return from Turkey, Daudi said it was discovered that what he had been facing wasn’t cancer.</p><p>Atleast there was  some bit of  improvement and was even walking again.</p><p>Sadly, it appears that his health issues resurfaced after his release from prison, where he expressed that he had gone through a tough experience.</p><p>While details about his funeral arrangements are still pending,  fellow musicians have gathered at the National Theatre to pay their respects.</p><p> </p><p>Read Also:<a href="https://info.ssegu.com/ugandan-musician-alien-skin-takes-on-key-role-in-new-political-party/">Ugandan Musician Alien Skin Takes On Key Role In New Political Party</a></p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><div>Content originally published on SSEGU.
