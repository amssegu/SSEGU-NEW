Aim for a structured workout routine that zeroes in on your shoulders, including key exercises like pull-ups, lateral raises, and overhead presses. For noticeable changes, commit to at least 3–4 shoulder-focused workouts per week. Start with manageable weights and reps, then steadily ramp up the intensity. If you’re effortlessly cranking out 10 pull-ups, it’s time to add some weight or aim for 15 reps next time. Your body's response to exercise is unique, and while some may see noticeable shoulder gains in a few months, others might take a bit longer. It’s vital to stay patient and committed to your workout and nutrition plan. Celebrate every bit of progress, even if it’s not as rapid as you hoped.
