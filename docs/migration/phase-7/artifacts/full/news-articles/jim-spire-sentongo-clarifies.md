<p> On June 22, the famous satirical cartoonist Jim Spire Sentongo announced the death of his elder brother who was also the heir of their father.</p><p>There has been disinformation saying that the deceased was shot dead and then dumped in a trench.</p><p>However, Jim Spire today clarified on this disinformation and many more concerning the death of his elder brother.</p><p>In his tweet, he says its not true that the deceased was shot and dumped in a trench, was not kidnapped by anyone for political or other related reasons.</p><p>Spire goes ahead to clarify that It is not true that he had ANY business relationship with Owek. Peter Mayiga. He was only a chief guest at the launch of Henrob Hospital in 2020  which Kiggundu built over the years through very hard work from a one room clinic in 2003 to where it is now. It is named after his sons Henry and Robert.</p><p>And he clarifies that the postmortem result shows that his brother died of Heart attack and was found a day after.</p><p>Dr. John Spire Kiggundu was a renowned obstetrician and gynecologist based in Kampala, Uganda, valued Director of Henrob Hospital Zzana.</p><p>He specialized in gynae-laparoscopy, a minimally invasive surgical technique used in women’s reproductive health.</p><p>Dr. John Spire Kiggundu will be laid to rest on Wednesday at his ancestral home in Butozi Masaka.</p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p>Read Also:<a href="https://info.ssegu.com/ragga-dee-claims-to-own-a-watch-worth-1-billion/">Ragga Dee Claims To Own a watch worth 1 Billion Ugandan Shillings</a></p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><div>Content originally published on SSEGU.
