This theory, known as Maslow's hierarchy of needs, outlines six different tiers of needs:</span></p><ol><li aria-level="1" dir="ltr"><p dir="ltr" role="presentation"><span>Physiological Needs:</span><span> Necessities for survival, such as air, water, food, and sleep.</span></p></li><li aria-level="1" dir="ltr"><p dir="ltr" role="presentation"><span>Safety Needs:</span><span> Security and safety, including housing, employment, and property.</span></p></li><li aria-level="1" dir="ltr"><p dir="ltr" role="presentation"><span>Love and Belongingness:</span><span> Relationships with friends, family, and intimate partners.</span></p></li><li aria-level="1" dir="ltr"><p dir="ltr" role="presentation"><span>Esteem:</span><span> Recognition, status, confidence, and respect from others.</span></p></li><li aria-level="1" dir="ltr"><p dir="ltr" role="presentation"><span>Self-Actualization:</span><span> Creativity, morality, problem-solving, and contribution to society.</span></p></li><li aria-level="1" dir="ltr"><p dir="ltr" role="presentation"><span>Self-transcendence:</span><span> True spirituality, enlightenment, unconditional love, and higher consciousness.</span></p></li></ol><p dir="ltr"><br></p><p dir="ltr"><span>The Real Purpose of Education: A Journey, Not a Destination</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span>Education should be the vehicle that helps you progress through these stages, ultimately achieving self-actualization and self-transcendence.
