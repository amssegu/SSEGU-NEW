<p>So the goal is to turn your skill into liquid cash fast, then use some of it to buy "battery" assets like gold, savings, or courses.</p>
<p>Here’s the exact blueprint:</p>

<h2>The 3-Phase Midas System</h2>
<p><strong>Phase 1: Cash Now</strong> = Turn skill into money in 7 days<br>
<strong>Phase 2: Leverage</strong> = Turn 1 hour into $500<br>
<strong>Phase 3: Passive</strong> = Money comes in while you sleep</p>

<h2>Phase 1: Get Liquid Cash Fast - Days 1-14</h2>
<p>Use these 3. They pay you this month.</p>
<p><strong>1. 1:1 Service</strong><br>
<strong>Do</strong>: "I will do X result for you for $Y"<br>
<strong>Example</strong>: Skill = Canva. Offer = "5 Instagram posts in 48 hours for $150"<br>
<strong>Where</strong>: DM 50 people, WhatsApp status, Facebook groups</p>
<p><strong>2. Local Service</strong><br>
<strong>Do</strong>: Sell in Bishkek first. Cash in hand.<br>
<strong>Example</strong>: Skill = English. Offer = "IELTS speaking practice $20/hour"<br>
<strong>Where</strong>: University groups, flyers, word of mouth</p>
<p><strong>3. Freelance Platforms</strong><br>
<strong>Do</strong>: Upwork, Fiverr, Telegram groups<br>
<strong>Example</strong>: Skill = Writing. Offer = "Product description in 24h - $50"<br>
<strong>Goal</strong>: Get 3 paid clients = $300-$900 liquid money</p>

<h2>Phase 2: Leverage Your Time - Days 15-45</h2>
<p>Now stop trading hours 1:1. Sell to groups.</p>
<p><strong>4. Group Workshop</strong><br>
<strong>Do</strong>: "Teach 10 people at once for 2 hours"<br>
<strong>Example</strong>: Skill = Cooking. "Samsa Masterclass - $25 per person"<br>
<strong>Math</strong>: 10 x $25 = $250 for 2 hours = $125/hour</p>
<p><strong>5. Productized Service</strong><br>
<strong>Do</strong>: Fixed price, fixed result, no custom work<br>
<strong>Example</strong>: Skill = Video editing. "1 Reel per week for $300/month"<br>
<strong>Why</strong>: Predictable money in. You can book 10 clients.</p>
<p><strong>6. Coaching Program</strong><br>
<strong>Do</strong>: 4 weeks, $500-$800 per person<br>
<strong>Example</strong>: Skill = Fitness. "8-Week Shred - $600"<br>
<strong>Why</strong>: Higher ticket = less clients needed</p>

<h2>Phase 3: Build Money Batteries - Days 46-90</h2>
<p>This is where money starts working without you. Now you can buy gold later.</p>
<p><strong>7. Digital Product</strong><br>
<strong>Do</strong>: Templates, checklists, guides<br>
<strong>Example</strong>: Skill = Tutoring. "100 Math Problems PDF - $19"<br>
<strong>Why</strong>: Make once, sell 100 times</p>
<p><strong>8. Online Course</strong><br>
<strong>Do</strong>: Record Zoom. Sell on Gumroad/Stan<br>
<strong>Example</strong>: Skill = Social media. "Grow to 1000 followers - $99"<br>
<strong>Why</strong>: $1000/month while you sleep</p>
<p><strong>9. Content + Affiliate</strong><br>
<strong>Do</strong>: Post tips about your skill. Link tools<br>
<strong>Example</strong>: Skill = Photography. "Best $100 camera" YouTube link<br>
<strong>Why</strong>: Free traffic pays you</p>
<p><strong>10. Licensing/Royalties</strong><br>
<strong>Do</strong>: Stock photos, music, fonts, ebook<br>
<strong>Example</strong>: Skill = Design. Sell icons on Creative Market<br>
<strong>Why</strong>: True passive</p>

<h2>What NOT To Do Yet</h2>
<p><strong>11. Sponsors/Content</strong> - Takes 6-12 months. Do this last.<br>
Focus on Phase 1 and 2 first to get liquid money power.</p>
