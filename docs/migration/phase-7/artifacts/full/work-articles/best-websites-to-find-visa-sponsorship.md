<p>Finding a job abroad with visa sponsorship and learning new skills for free used to take months of research. In 2026, you can do both online from your phone.</p>
<p>The challenge is knowing which websites are real and which are scams. This guide gives you the best websites to find visa sponsorship jobs and free courses with certificates that actually help you get hired.</p>
<p>Bookmark this page. These are the official, trusted sites recruiters and governments use.</p>
<h2>Part 1: Best Websites to Find Visa Sponsorship Jobs</h2>
<p>These sites list real employers who are legally allowed to sponsor foreign workers.</p>
<h2>1. Government Job Portals</h2>
<p>Government sites are the safest place to start. Jobs here are from approved employers.</p>
<p><strong>Canada Job Bank</strong>: jobbank.gc.ca</p>
<p>Filter "LMIA available" to see jobs with sponsorship. High demand in farming, healthcare, construction, and hospitality.</p>
<p><strong>UK Find a Job + UKVI Sponsor List</strong>: findajob.dwp.gov.uk and gov.uk/find-a-licensed-sponsor</p>
<p>Only apply to companies on the UKVI list. They can legally sponsor Skilled Worker visas.</p>
<p><strong>Australia JobActive and Seek</strong>: jobsearch.gov.au and seek.com.au</p>
<p>Search "482 visa sponsorship". Australia sponsors nurses, chefs, mechanics, and farm workers.</p>
<p><strong>USA SeasonalJobs.dol.gov</strong></p>
<p>Official US government site for H-2A farm and H-2B seasonal hotel jobs with sponsorship.</p>
<p><strong>Germany Make it in Germany</strong>: make-it-in-germany.com</p>
<p>Lists jobs for skilled workers and the new Opportunity Card. Nursing and engineering roles are open.</p>
<h2>2. Global Job Boards With Visa Filters</h2>
<p>These sites let you filter for "visa sponsorship" or "relocation".</p>
<p><strong>Indeed.com</strong></p>
<p>Search "visa sponsorship" + job title + country. Example: "visa sponsorship nurse UK"</p>
<p><strong>LinkedIn Jobs</strong>: linkedin.com/jobs</p>
<p>Use filters: "Easy Apply" and keywords "visa sponsorship". Connect with recruiters directly.</p>
<p><strong>Glassdoor</strong></p>
<p>See salary, reviews, and if the company has sponsored before.</p>
<h2>3. Websites That Track H-1B and Skilled Sponsors</h2>
<p><strong>MyVisaJobs.com</strong></p>
<p>Database of US companies that sponsored H-1B and green cards. See who hired foreigners last year.</p>
<p><strong>H1BGrader.com</strong></p>
<p>Check salary and approval rates for US tech sponsors.</p>
<h2>4. Gulf and Middle East Job Sites</h2>
<p><strong>Bayt.com</strong></p>
<p>Largest job site for UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar. Most jobs include visa, housing, and flights.</p>
<p><strong>Gulftalent.com</strong></p>
<p>Professional jobs in finance, engineering, and hospitality with sponsorship.</p>
<h2>5. Industry-Specific Job Boards</h2>
<p><strong>Hospitality</strong>: hcareers.com, catererglobal.com</p>
<p><strong>Healthcare</strong>: nhscareers.nhs.uk for UK, healthforceontario.ca for Canada</p>
<p><strong>Agriculture</strong>: agrirecruit.ca for Canada farms</p>
<p><strong>Teaching</strong>: tefl.com, searchassociates.com</p>

<h2>How to Avoid Scams on These Websites</h2>
<p>Even on good sites, scammers post fake jobs.</p>
<p>1. <strong>Never pay</strong>: Real jobs do not ask for money. Government sites are free.</p>
<p>2. <strong>Check company on sponsor list</strong>: For UK and Australia, verify on government list.</p>
<p>3. <strong>Use company email</strong>: Real HR uses @company.com, not @gmail.com</p>
<p>4. <strong>Do video interview</strong>: No interview = red flag.</p>
<p>5. <strong>Free courses are free</strong>: If a site asks you to pay before watching, leave.</p>

<p><strong>2. Which site is best for beginners?</strong></p>
<p>For jobs: Job Bank Canada and LinkedIn. For courses: Google Digital Garage and Microsoft Learn.</p>
<p><strong>3. Can I get a job with only a free certificate?</strong></p>
<p>Yes for entry jobs. Combine certificate + 1 project + applications.</p>
<p><strong>4. Do I need English for these sites?</strong></p>
<p>Most job and course sites are in English. Some have other languages.</p>
<p><strong>5. How do I know if a job on LinkedIn offers sponsorship?</strong></p>
<p>Use keywords: "visa sponsorship", "work visa", "relocation assistance" in search.</p>
