<p> Kampala’s well-known socialite, Christian Asiimwe, affectionately called Don Chris, is currently on the run after being accused of defrauding over 100 Ugandans in a job scam that promised opportunities abroad.</p><p>This situation has left many individuals feeling quite upset, as it is believed he has taken off with more than 500 million shillings.</p><p>Known for his lavish lifestyle and love for music events, Don Chris is reportedly hiding out abroad, in an attempt to avoid legal troubles.</p><p>In August 2023, Don Chris founded Skypins Tours and Travel Company, which he marketed as an immigration firm.</p><p>The company quickly gained attention through ads on various media platforms, claiming to provide Ugandans with job opportunities mainly in Europe and America.</p><p>Despite his advice against selling valuables or taking loans for fees, emphasizing the uncertainty of embassy approvals, many people, desperate for a better life, chose to ignore his caution. Video circulating online highlights  claims of Don defrauding the people.</p><blockquote><p dir="ltr" lang="en">Hundreds of people have today stormed Don Chris’ SkyPin offices in Kitende demanding refunds of monies he is said to have collected from them claiming he would provide employment abroad.</p><p>Don Chris is said to have fled the country and is in hiding. <a href="https://t.co/j1w8wF22ud">pic.twitter.com/j1w8wF22ud</a></p><p>— BigEyeUG (@BigEyeUG) <a href="https://twitter.com/BigEyeUG/status/1942294434478948813?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw">July 7, 2025</a></p></blockquote><p></p><p>The excitement around these advertisements led many hopefulv to Skypins Tours and Travel Company at Haruna Mall in Ntinda, unaware that the dreams they harbored would soon turn into a heartbreaking experience.</p><p>The Criminal Investigations Directorate (CID) began looking into the scam on June 17, 2025, initially identifying 85 victims, although many believe the true number is larger.</p><p>Unfortunately, Skypins was unhelpful when it came to issuing refunds, which led the police to undertake a manhunt for Don Chris.</p><p>Additionally, the Ministry of Gender and Labour has pointed out that Skypins never held a license to operate as an external labor recruitment agency, further revealing the deceitful nature of this scheme.</p><p> </p><p>Read Also: <a href="https://info.ssegu.com/dax-vibez-released-and-police-explains-earlier-arrest/">Dax Vibes Released out And Police Explains  His  Earlier Arrest. </a></p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><div>Content originally published on SSEGU.
