# 50-course catalogue shortlist: employability skills

**Prepared:** 22 November 2026  
**Audience:** International, with Uganda as the primary market  
**Purpose:** Internal shortlist for a curated catalogue that links learners to the course provider. The courses are not offered by SSEGU.

## How to use this shortlist

This is a curated starting point, not a claim that one certificate guarantees work or is formally recognized by every employer. The selection favors courses from universities, public institutions, and established technology or professional organizations; practical assignments; useful skills across multiple industries; and a mix of free learning and paid credentials. It includes technical and non-technical routes, plus subjects with practical relevance in Uganda and elsewhere.

The course provider’s page is the source of truth for availability, fees, certificate terms, prerequisites, country access, dates, language, and estimated workload. These details change. Check them before publishing a listing and again before sending a learner to enroll. In particular, do not describe a course as free if only its preview or audit content is free.

## Understanding the cost labels

- **Free:** Core learning is available at no cost. Some providers also give a completion record or badge for free.
- **Free learning; paid credential/exam:** Learners can study at no cost; they pay only if they want an assessed certificate or proctored certification exam. The learning and the credential are separate products.
- **Audit/free access where offered; paid certificate:** Some or most course material may be available free, but graded work, projects, or a shareable certificate may require payment. Availability varies by course and location.
- **Paid certificate/program:** The full structured program and verified credential normally require payment. A preview, trial, scholarship, or financial aid may be available, but should not be promised without checking the current page.

Coursera lists Coursera Plus at US$59/month in the US at the time of research; the local price and promotions can differ. Google says Career Certificates cost US$49/month in the US and Canada and directs learners elsewhere to check local Coursera enrollment pricing. edX says verified certificate fees vary by course. Prices below therefore identify the payment model and the benefit of paying, rather than inventing a Uganda-specific checkout price. [Coursera Plus pricing](https://www.coursera.org/courseraplus) · [Google Career Certificates pricing](https://grow.google/certificates/) · [edX course costs](https://support.edx.org/hc/en-us/articles/206211958-What-does-it-cost-to-take-a-course)

## What each cost tier gives the learner

| Tier | What the learner gets | When it is worth recommending |
|---|---|---|
| **Free learning** | Course content, practice, and sometimes a free badge or completion certificate. No financial barrier; often enough to build a skill and a sample project. | Recommend for exploration, foundational skills, and where the platform already gives a verifiable free credential (e.g., SkillsBuild, HubSpot Academy, Skillshop, freeCodeCamp, Saylor, or eligible OpenLearn/FAO courses). |
| **Paid certificate or verified track** | Assessed work, graded assignments, a provider-issued credential, and sometimes portfolio projects or identity verification. On edX, free audit generally omits the verified credential and some graded features; its verified fee varies by course. | Recommend when the learner needs structured assessment or a credential they can share, and the issuing organization is relevant to the target role. Verify the exact Uganda price first. |
| **Paid vendor certification exam** | A proctored exam from a technology vendor (e.g., Microsoft, AWS, Salesforce, Cisco) that validates knowledge against a defined certification standard. It is separate from free training. | Recommend after the learner has practiced and is targeting jobs that request that certification. Avoid urging every beginner to pay for an exam before they know the subject suits them. |
| **Paid subscription/program** | A sequenced curriculum, graded tasks, labs/projects, instructor-designed pathway, and completion credential. One subscription can cover multiple courses, but monthly billing can add up. | Recommend if the learner plans to finish promptly, can afford the full likely duration, and wants the structured route. Compare the local monthly cost against a single-course option and check financial aid. |

## Editorial rules for turning this into public catalogue entries

1. **Use the provider as the source of truth.** Link directly to the official enrollment page, not an aggregator or a third-party copy.
2. **Show two costs separately:** learning access and credential/exam cost. Use “free course” only when the learner can actually access the learning at no charge; label audit limits.
3. **State what the credential is.** A completion certificate, digital badge, vendor certification, academic credit recommendation, and accredited qualification are different things.
4. **Add a Uganda access check.** Record whether the course enrolls learners in Uganda, accepts available payment methods, works on mobile/low bandwidth, and has local pricing or financial aid. Keep an international-access note where relevant.
5. **Describe a concrete outcome.** Say what the learner will practice or make, rather than promising a job or salary.
6. **Keep the list selective.** A quality catalogue should include a few choices per career pathway, not every course a provider lists. Recheck dead links, changed fees, retired courses, and certificate terms on a schedule.
