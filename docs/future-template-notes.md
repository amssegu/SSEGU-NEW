# Future Template Boundary

Main-site components remain under `src/components/main`; genuinely reusable
foundations belong under `src/components/shared` or `src/components/ui`.

The Phase 4 amendment now permits the independent fixture Work namespace under
`src/components/work`, `src/layouts/WorkLayout.astro`, `src/styles/work.css` and
`/work/` routes. Work presentation remains separate from the News visual system.
CMS content, migration data, employer submissions, accounts and production
redirects remain deferred to their approved later phases.
