<p>1. <strong>100% free</strong>: No fees, no trials, no hidden costs</p>
<p>2. <strong>Self-paced</strong>: Learn anytime from your phone or laptop</p>
<p>3. <strong>Learn by doing</strong>: You code directly in the browser. No setup needed</p>
<p>4. <strong>Project-based</strong>: You build 5 projects for every certification</p>
<p>5. <strong>Certificates</strong>: You earn a verified certificate after completing projects</p>
<p>Over 1 million people have used freeCodeCamp to get their first job in tech.</p>
