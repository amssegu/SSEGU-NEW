<h2><b><span>Identifying Your Skills and Niche</span></b></h2><p><span>When starting a freelance business as a side hustle, it's important to first identify your skills and niche. Take some time to assess your strengths and interests to determine what services you can offer as a freelancer. Consider your previous work experience, hobbies, and any specialized knowledge you may have. This will help you narrow down your focus and target a specific market.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>Once you've identified your skills, it's crucial to find your niche. This involves understanding your target audience and the specific needs you can fulfill. Research the market and identify areas where there is a demand for your skills.
