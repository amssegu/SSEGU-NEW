<p>Free courses let you test accounting before investing in a degree. You can try bookkeeping, financial analysis, or tax and see what fits your career goals, all at zero cost.</p>

<h2>FAQ: Free Accounting Courses With Certificates</h2>
<p><strong>1. Are these certificates really free?</strong></p>
<p>Yes. Alison, Great Learning, and QuickBooks offer 100% free certificates. Coursera and LinkedIn are free with trial or financial aid.</p>
<p><strong>2. Do I need a degree to take these courses?</strong></p>
<p>No. Most courses are for beginners. No prior accounting experience needed.</p>
<p><strong>3. How long does it take to complete?</strong></p>
<p>From 3 hours to 4 months. You can finish LinkedIn Foundations in one weekend. Google Bookkeeping takes 3 to 4 months.</p>
<p><strong>4. Which is better, bookkeeping or accounting?</strong></p>
<p>Bookkeeping is daily recording of transactions. Accounting includes reporting and analysis. Start with bookkeeping if you want a job fast.</p>
<p><strong>5. Can I get multiple certificates?</strong></p>
<p>Yes. It is smart. Aim for 2 certificates in 60 days. Example: Google Bookkeeping + QuickBooks.</p>
<p><strong>6. Do I need to buy accounting software?</strong></p>
<p>No. QuickBooks, Tally, and Excel have free trials. Many courses provide practice files.</p>
