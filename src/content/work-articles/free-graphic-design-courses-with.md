<h2>Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>
<p>1. <strong>Only watching tutorials</strong>. You learn design by doing. Complete every project.</p>
<p>2. <strong>Not building a portfolio</strong>. Certificates alone are not enough. Show your work.</p>
<p>3. <strong>Copying designs</strong>. Learn principles, then create original work.</p>
<p>4. <strong>Ignoring feedback</strong>. Join design communities and ask for critiques.</p>

<h2>FAQ: Free Graphic Design Courses With Certificates</h2>
<p><strong>1. Are these certificates really free?</strong></p>
<p>Yes. Canva, Adobe, and Great Learning offer 100% free certificates. Google, Coursera, and LinkedIn are free with trial or financial aid.</p>
<p><strong>2. Do I need to buy Photoshop or Illustrator?</strong></p>
<p>No. Canva, Figma, and Adobe Express are free. Many courses teach you using free tools.</p>
<p><strong>3. How long does it take to complete?</strong></p>
<p>From 2 hours to 6 months. You can finish Canva in one day. Google UX takes 3 to 6 months.</p>
<p><strong>4. Which is better, Canva or Adobe?</strong></p>
<p>Canva is best for fast social media and marketing design. Adobe is best for professional branding and print. Learn both.</p>
<p><strong>5. Can I get multiple certificates?</strong></p>
<p>Yes. It is smart. Aim for 2 certificates in 60 days. Example: Google UX + Canva Design.</p>
<p><strong>6. Do I need drawing skills?</strong></p>
<p>No. Graphic design is about communication, not drawing. You can use templates, icons, and photos.</p>
