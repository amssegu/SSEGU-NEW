<p>Excel is still the #1 skill employers ask for in 2026. From finance and marketing to HR and data analysis, every company uses Excel to track data, make reports, and make decisions.</p>
<p>The good news is you can learn Excel for free. Microsoft, Google, Coursera, LinkedIn, and top universities offer free Excel courses with certificates that you can add to your resume and LinkedIn.</p>
<p>Whether you are a complete beginner or want to master formulas, pivot tables, and dashboards, this guide covers the best free Excel courses with certificates, what you will learn, and how to use your certificate to get a job or promotion.</p>
<h2>Why Free Excel Courses With Certificates Matter</h2>
<p>Excel is not just for accountants. 90% of jobs use Excel in some way. Hiring managers test for it in interviews.</p>
<p>A certificate proves you completed training and know how to use Excel. When the certificate is from Microsoft or Google, it carries weight because these are the companies that build the tools.</p>
<p>Free courses let you build skills without paying for software or college. You can learn at your own pace and prove your skills to employers immediately.</p>

<p><strong>1. Add it to LinkedIn and your resume</strong></p>
<p>Example: Excel Essential Training, LinkedIn Learning, 2026. Link the certificate.</p>
<p><strong>2. Build 2 to 3 Excel projects</strong></p>
<p>Create a sales dashboard, budget tracker, and data report. Share them in your portfolio.</p>
<p><strong>3. Combine 2 certificates</strong></p>
<p>Microsoft Excel + Google Data Analytics shows you know both tools and analysis.</p>
<p><strong>4. Use it in interviews</strong></p>
<p>Talk about how you used formulas and pivot tables to solve a problem. That matters more than the certificate.</p>

<h2>FAQ: Free Excel Courses With Certificates Online</h2>
<p><strong>1. Are these certificates really free?</strong></p>
<p>Yes. Microsoft, Alison, and Great Learning offer 100% free certificates. Coursera and LinkedIn are free with trial or financial aid.</p>
<p><strong>2. Do I need Microsoft Excel installed?</strong></p>
<p>No. You can use Excel Online for free. Most courses also work with Google Sheets.</p>
<p><strong>3. How long does it take to complete?</strong></p>
<p>From 2 hours to 1 month. You can finish Microsoft Fundamentals in one afternoon. Full specializations take longer.</p>
<p><strong>4. Which is better, Microsoft or Google certificate?</strong></p>
<p>Microsoft is best for pure Excel skills. Google is best if you want to combine Excel with data analytics.</p>
<p><strong>5. Can I get multiple certificates?</strong></p>
<p>Yes. It is smart. Aim for 2 certificates in 30 days. Example: Microsoft Fundamentals + LinkedIn Essential.</p>
<p><strong>6. Is Excel still relevant with AI and Google Sheets?</strong></p>
<p>Yes. 80% of companies still use Excel. AI tools work inside Excel now, so skills are even more valuable.</p>
