<p>1. <strong>Filters for free certificates</strong>: You can search specifically for courses that are 100% free with a certificate</p>

<h2>How to Use Class Central to Find Free Certificate Courses</h2>
<p><strong>Step 1: Go to classcentral.com</strong></p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Use the "Free Certificate" filter</strong></p>
<p>On the left sidebar, click "Price" and select "Free". Then click "Certificate" and select "Yes".</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Filter by subject and provider</strong></p>
<p>Example: Subject = "Data Science", Provider = "Google" or "Harvard"</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Check "Full Course, Free Certificate" in description</strong></p>
<p>Some courses are free to audit but charge for the certificate. Class Central flags the ones that are 100% free.</p>
