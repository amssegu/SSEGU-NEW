<h2>How to Make the Most of Free Beginner Courses</h2>
<p>Finishing the course is only half the work. Here is how to turn learning into results.</p>
<p><strong>1. Set a schedule</strong></p>
<p>30 minutes a day is better than 5 hours on Sunday. Be consistent.</p>
<p><strong>2. Take notes and practice</strong></p>
<p>Write down key ideas. Do every exercise, even if it feels easy.</p>
<p><strong>3. Build 1 small project</strong></p>
<p>Marketing: make a social post. Excel: make a budget. Coding: make a calculator.</p>
<p><strong>4. Add certificate to LinkedIn</strong></p>
<p>Go to "Licenses &amp; Certifications" and add it. Recruiters search for these.</p>
<p><strong>5. Tell people what you learned</strong></p>
<p>Post once on LinkedIn. "I just finished Google Digital Marketing. Here is 1 thing I learned..."</p>
<h2>Common Mistakes Beginners Make</h2>
<p>1. <strong>Starting 5 courses at once</strong>. Finish one, then move on.</p>
<p>2. <strong>Only watching videos</strong>. Pause and do the work.</p>
<p>3. <strong>Waiting to be "ready"</strong>. You learn by doing, not by waiting.</p>
<p>4. <strong>Not applying skills</strong>. Use what you learn at work, in school, or for a project.</p>
