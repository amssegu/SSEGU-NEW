<p><strong>1. Liquidity</strong><br>
Money is cash, card, bank transfer. You can spend $10 in 2 seconds.<br>
Gold and diamonds? You have to find a buyer, test it, negotiate, get paid later. That takes days.</p>
<p><strong>2. Universal Acceptance</strong><br>
The store, the landlord, the app, the government all take money. 
Try paying rent in a diamond. Most people will say no.</p>
<p><strong>3. Divisibility</strong><br>
You can pay $3.47 exactly with money.<br>
You cannot cut a gold bar or a diamond into $3.47 pieces without destroying value.</p>
<p><strong>4. Backed By Law + Trust</strong><br>
Money works because the government says "this is legal tender" and we all agree on it.<br>
Gold and diamonds work because we agree they are rare and pretty. But that agreement breaks in a crisis when you need bread.</p>
<p><strong>5. No Storage Hassle</strong><br>
$10,000 in money fits on your phone.  
$10,000 in gold is heavy and needs a safe.  
$10,000 in diamonds is tiny but you need an expert to verify it.</p>

<h2>But Gold and Diamonds Win In 3 Ways</h2>

<tbody><tr><td><strong>Category</strong></td><td><strong>Money</strong></td><td><strong>Gold/Diamonds</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Inflation</strong></td><td><strong>Loses value over time</strong></td><td><strong>Holds value for 100 years</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Portability</strong></td><td><strong>Need bank/internet</strong></td><td><strong>Physical, no bank needed</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Crisis</strong></td><td><strong>Can be frozen or devalued</strong></td><td><strong>Still traded worldwide</strong></td></tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>That is why rich people own all 3. Money for power today. Gold for power in 20 years.</p>

<h2>The Real Power Difference</h2>
<p><strong>Money = Power to act now</strong>  
Pay employees, buy ads, invest, eat. It is command over other people’s time and products.</p>
<p><strong>Gold/Diamonds = Power to store</strong>  
They do not do anything. They just sit there and keep value. You have to convert them to money first to use them.</p>
<p>So money is more "powerful" because power = ability to get things done. And money is the universal remote control for getting things done.</p>

<p><strong>Think of it like this:</strong><br>
Gold is a battery. Money is electricity.  
The battery stores energy. But only electricity runs the house.</p>
