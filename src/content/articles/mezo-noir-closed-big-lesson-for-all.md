Martha reportedly collapsed after consuming alcohol under suspicious circumstances at Mezo Noir. Sources who have reviewed surveillance footage say she was last seen entering the club’s office with a man identified as Eddy.</p><p>Moments later, witnesses claim, Eddy emerged from the office in a panic and returned with other individuals.
