<h2><b><span>The Role of Testosterone in the Body</span></b></h2><p><span>Testosterone is a hormone that plays a crucial role in the development and maintenance of male reproductive tissues.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>It is responsible for various functions such as the growth of facial and body hair, deepening of the voice, and muscle development.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>Testosterone also plays a role in mood regulation, bone density, and overall energy levels in both men and women.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>Maintaining optimal testosterone levels is important for overall health and well-being.</span></p><p></p><div><a Testosterone Levels</span></b></p><p><span>Testosterone levels can vary among individuals and can be influenced by various factors such as age, genetics, and lifestyle choices.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>Normal testosterone levels in adult men range from 300 to 1,000 nanograms per deciliter (ng/dL), while in adult women, the levels are much lower,
