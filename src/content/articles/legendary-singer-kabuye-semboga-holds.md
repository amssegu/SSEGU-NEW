<p> <span>Semboga, a legendary Ugandan singeler, songwriter, and audio producer, celebrated an impressive 42 years in his music career with a spectacular concert. On May 23, 2025, this iconic event took place at the Serena Hotel, organized by Balunywa Promotions. Attendees were treated to a live band performance that showcased Kabuye’s beloved hits, including “Ebisanyi,” “Kangume,” “Sheila,” and “Fahima.”</span></p><p>The concert attracted a remarkable lineup of notable guests and artists, including Mariam Ndagire and singers like Carol Natongo, Bruno K, Betty Mpologoma, Willy Mukabya, Eddy Yawe, Mary Bata, and Nandor Love, all of whom contributed to the vibrant atmosphere through Legetheir music. Fans were energized, singing along passionately as Kabuye took the stage, highlighting his incredible ability to connect with his audience.</p><p>Kabuye is renowned for his innovative fusion of traditional and modern music, earning him the title of the Father of Real Music in Uganda over his four-decade-long career.
