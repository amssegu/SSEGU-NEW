<p> Former Ugandan media personality Anita Fabiola and her husband, Mark Ronald Mubiru, are celebrating the arrival of twins, a momentous occasion that has captivated their fans. The couple, who exchanged vows during a lavish introduction ceremony in January 2023, kept the pregnancy a closely guarded secret, making the recent announcement a delightful shock to the public.</p><p>The news broke on Tuesday, June 17, 2025, when Anitah Fabiola shared the exciting update on social media, just four days after giving birth. In an emotional post, she revealed, “We are overjoyed to share our double blessing with you. With hearts full of love and gratitude, we welcome our beautiful twins into the world. Seth &amp; Rumi born 13/06/25 perfect in every way and already filling our lives with so much love. Thank you for your love, prayers, and support as we begin this amazing new chapter as a family.”</p><p><span>A heartwarming hospital photo , depicting the proud parents cradling their newborns amidst colourful balloons, and the new ones covered in  light Pink and light Blue Cloths</span></p>[caption id="attachment_6337" align="aligncenter" width="300"]<em>Anita Fabiola, Her Husband And their twins(COURTESY PHOTO)</em>[/caption]<p>In Buganda culture, the birth of twins is a prestigious event, earning the father the title Ssalongo and the mother Nalongo.</p><p>The timing of the birth adds an extra layer of celebration for Anitah, whose birthday falls in June.
