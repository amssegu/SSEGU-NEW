<h2><b><span>Understanding the relationship between subscribers and views</span></b></h2><p><span>When it comes to YouTube's success, the number of subscribers plays a crucial role in determining the views on a channel. As the subscriber count increases, the potential for views also increases.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>The relationship between subscribers and views can be explained by the fact that subscribers are more likely to watch the videos uploaded by a channel. This leads to higher view counts as subscribers are more likely to engage with the videos and share them with others.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>Additionally, subscribers contribute to the overall visibility of a YouTube channel. When a subscriber watches a video, it increases the likelihood of the video being recommended to other users.
