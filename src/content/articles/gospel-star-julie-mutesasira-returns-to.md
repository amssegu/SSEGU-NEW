<p> After spending several years in Canada, beloved Ugandan gospel singer — Julie Birungi aka. <strong>Julie Mutesasira </strong> has finally touched down in Kampala, sending her fans into a frenzy! The <em><strong>"Nkwagala"</strong></em> hitmaker was all smiles as she arrived at Entebbe International Airport, looking radiant.</p><p>Sources close to the singer reveal that Julie is back for both personal and professional reasons—hinting at possible new music, ministry engagements, and reconnecting with family. — <strong><em>"Uganda is my home, and I’ve missed my people so much," </em></strong>she reportedly told friends at the airport. — <em><strong>"It’s time to come back and share the love of God in person, not just through songs."</strong></em></p><p> </p><p>Julie, who relocated to Canada years ago, has been quietly balancing music, ministry, and family life abroad.
