You need to change your limiting beliefs and have clarity about why you want to become rich by creating a life purpose. While the previous content focused on the inner game, this episode will approach the topic by giving you practical steps that will help you become wealthy.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span>Before we start discussing these steps, I must tell you that this is not a formula nor a get-rich-quick scheme. You must become a valuable person. Here's how it works: as a general truth, people pay in exchange for what you have to offer. Let's assume you have a regular job, and you are the department manager of a decent clothing store. In that case, you will be paid in exchange for your services and your time. Your salary is higher than that of one of your cashiers because you have a leading position that demands more responsibilities, more stress, and more knowledge. You get paid for what you know, your ability to manage, and your performance.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span>People who are closer to the top of the hierarchy get a better salary than those at the bottom. This logic is simple: if you invest in yourself, you can offer more, and therefore you get a better paycheck in exchange for your knowledge and abilities.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span>When I say invest in yourself, I refer to both time and money. One must invest time before anything else because it takes time to develop a new skill or improve an existing ability. You need to be willing to invest your time. Only after you become good at something will people start to pay for your services.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span>Attending seminars, reading books, and watching self-help YouTube videos are just a few ways you can become more valuable. Strive to improve your skills and deliver great products or services to others. However, assuming most of you believe that being rich means becoming a millionaire, a regular job will not satisfy your desire. To illustrate this, consider that you are the department manager of a clothing store. There are a limited number of hours you can work per month, and your earnings are tied to the amount of time you work. Unless you have a high-paying job, you won't become rich this way.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span>The only solution left is to become an entrepreneur. Running your own business allows you to create a passive income, so you are not limited by the number of hours you can work each day. Passive income generates profit regardless of where you are or what you are doing.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span>However, being an entrepreneur is not as easy as it sounds. That person could earn over $20,000 a month but easily waste that money on the latest gadgets due to their inability to resist their desires.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span>Learning how to manage your finances properly is crucial. If you waste your money on gambling or unnecessary purchases, that money is not an asset anymore. Instead, you need to change before you make the money.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span>Spending time with people who inspire you and have something to teach you brings massive benefits.
