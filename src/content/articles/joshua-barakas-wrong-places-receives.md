<p> Joshua Baraka's latest single, "Wrong Places," is making waves in the music world,  so far reaching  5 million streams in just under a month since its release on May 16, 2025.</p><p>This  milestone highlights Baraka’s growing influence in the global music scene and showcases the incredible talent coming out of  Uganda.</p><p>The song involving a mix of different musical styles that really resonates with listeners, showcasing Baraka’s talent for authentic storytelling and top-notch production.</p><p>Sharing his excitement on social media, Baraka expresses his heartfelt gratitude to his fans.</p><p>"5 MILLION STREAMS in less than a month! 🙌🏽🎉 I’m beyond grateful for every listen, every share, and every message. Wrong Places came from a real place and seeing it connect with so many of you means everything. Thank you for this milestone. Let’s keep going! ❤️"</p><p>With the talented JAE5 at the helm of production, known for his work with many artists, the track’s polished sound has definitely shaped  it reach to  a wide audience.</p><p>Released under MOVES Recordings, a  label that has played a key role in Baraka’s rapid rise to fame in the industry.</p><p>Born on December 13, 2000, in Kawempe, Uganda, Joshua Baraka is a vibrant Ugandan recording artist and music producer who is gaining international recognition at a faster speed.</p><p>His journey really took off with his hit "Nana" in 2023, which marked a turning point in his career.</p><p>He has  enchanted audiences with over ten albums, including “Baby Step,” “Belinda,” and “Sana,” each one a testament to his versatility and growth as an artist.</p><p>Baraka is also making an impact as a recognized RADAR Africa artist on Spotify, a program that champions emerging talent from the continent.</p><p>The  positive response to "Wrong Places" shows just how deeply he connects with his fans, and a bright future ahead to win Awards both local and Global.</p><p>Read Also: <a href="https://info.ssegu.com/emmanuel-bwiire-of-benti-boys-reported-missing-duo-partner-appeals-for-help/">Emmanuel Bwiire of Benti Boys Reported Missing, Duo Partner Appeals for Help</a></p><p> </p><p> </p><div>Content originally published on SSEGU.
