While its title and much of the text concern increased income, Hill insists the philosophy taught can help people succeed in any line of work. The Secret by Rhonda Byrne</span></span></h3><p dir="ltr"><span><span>When to Read:</span><span> When you've never read a business book but want to be successful.</span></span></p><p dir="ltr"><span><span>Though we’ve said positive thinking alone isn't enough, this book is a great starting point for beginners. The Intelligent Investor by Benjamin Graham</span></span></h3><p dir="ltr"><span><span>When to Read:</span><span> When considering investing in the stock market.</span></span></p><p dir="ltr"><span><span>Graham, the mentor and inspiration of Warren Buffett, teaches the vital elements of value investing. Money: Master the Game by Tony Robbins</span></span></h3><p dir="ltr"><span><span>When to Read:</span><span> When money starts coming in and you want to know what to do with it.</span></span></p><p dir="ltr"><span><span>Tony Robbins leverages his connections to share strategies of the super-wealthy. It’s a rare book that redefines conventional wisdom.</span></span></p><span><br></span><h3 dir="ltr"><span><span>7.
