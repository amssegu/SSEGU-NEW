<p> Ugandan singer Dax Vibez has confirmed that his highly anticipated All White Concert is still on track, despite a recent setback, after Hotel Africana abruptly canceled his scheduled performance.</p><p>Dax Vibez has now  moved the event to UMA Showgrounds in Lugogo. The concert, originally planned for August 29, 2025, will now proceed on the same date at the new venue, as announced by the artist himself.</p><p>In a tweet posted, Dax Vibez shared the updated plans with an enthusiastic message,“Same date, better location, new generation tugenda ku UMA,” accompanied by a vibrant poster.</p><blockquote><p dir="ltr" lang="en">Same date, New Location. Better Experience. 29th August.
