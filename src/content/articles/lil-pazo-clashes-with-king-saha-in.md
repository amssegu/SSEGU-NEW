King Saha, the vocal titan, just turned Lugogo Cricket Oval into a fan-packed frenzy—think matatu rush hour but with screaming teens and flashing phones. He’s the king of the scene, trending like crazy, with a voice so smooth it could sell ice to a polar bear. But don’t let those golden pipes fool you—Saha’s beef history is wilder than a boda boda dodging potholes. He’s tangled with Bebe Cool, Gravity Omutujju, and more, yet still reigns supreme. Pazo’s still at it, dissecting Saha like a detective, while fans are in stitches. “Pazo’s mouth’s faster than his career!” one stitched.
