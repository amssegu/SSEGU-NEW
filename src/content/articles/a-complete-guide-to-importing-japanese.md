less): 40% import duty.</span></p></li><li aria-level="2" dir="ltr"><p dir="ltr" role="presentation"><span>Buses and minivans (vehicles carrying 10 or more people): up to 60% import dir="ltr"><p dir="ltr" role="presentation"><span>This tax is calculated on the vehicle's total value, including all incurred fees such as brokerage commissions and transport costs from the country of origin.</span></p></li></ul><li aria-level="1" dir="ltr"><p dir="ltr" role="presentation"><span>Surtax on Older Vehicles</span></p></li><ul><li aria-level="2" dir="ltr"><p dir="ltr" Determination</span></h4><p dir="ltr"><span face="Arial, sans-serif">The Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA) physically examines each imported vehicle to assess its value. They have the authority to accept or reject the declared value provided by the importer or clearing agent. The evaluation considers the open market value of identical cars, past accepted values for similar models, and the vehicle's condition at the time of importation. This process ensures that imported goods are not undervalued or overvalued. Importers can appeal the valuation if dissatisfied.</span></p><br><h4 dir="ltr"><span face="Arial, sans-serif">Vehicle Duty Value</span></h4><p dir="ltr"><span face="Arial, sans-serif">The duty on imported Japanese cars is calculated based on the total import cost, including purchase, importation, freight, and delivery charges.
