<p> Ugandan musician Dax Vibez, whose real name is Ivan Bugembe Sentamu, took to social media  to announce the cancellation of his highly anticipated maiden concert. “After 9 months of advertising and all, Hotel Africana has cancelled my show. I don’t know what to do next,” he wrote, leaving fans shocked.</p><blockquote><p dir="ltr" lang="en">After 9 months of advertising and all, Hotel Africana has cancelled my show.</p><p>I don’t know what to do next.</p><p>— DAX VIBEZ (@Daxvibes) <a href="https://twitter.com/Daxvibes/status/1938994376664915982?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw">June 28, 2025</a></p></blockquote><p></p><p>Dax Vibez, the younger brother of opposition leader and musician Bobi Wine (Robert Kyagulanyi Ssentamu), had been planning the event for August 29, 2025, at Hotel Africana in Kampala.</p><p>The decision to host the concert was first announced in February 2025, marking nine months of promotion that now appear jeopardized.</p><p>Hotel Africana, a prominent venue known for hosting major artists, has a mixed history with event cancellations, as seen with Alien Skin’s concert relocation in February 2025.</p><p>Dax Vibez now together with his management have to look for another location just two months to the maiden concert.</p><p> </p><p> </p><p>Read Also:<a href="https://info.ssegu.com/bobi-wines-son-shadrack-visits-eddie-mutwe-in-prison/">Bobi Wine's Son Shadrack Visits Eddie Mutwe In Prison.</a></p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><div>Content originally published on SSEGU.
