<p>You do not need a loan. You do not need employees.</p>
<p>You need $100, 1 skill, and people who hate doing the task.</p>
<p>In 2027, local service businesses still print cash because people pay for convenience.</p>
<p><strong>The $100 Rule</strong>: If you can solve it with your hands + phone, you can start it.</p>
<h2>How To Start Any Service With $100</h2>
<p>1. <strong>$30</strong>: Basic tools or supplies</p>
<p>2. <strong>$30</strong>: Flyers + Google Business Profile</p>
<p>3. <strong>$40</strong>: Facebook/WhatsApp ads or fuel to get to clients</p>
<p>Goal: First 3 customers = profit</p>
<h2>The 20 Service Businesses Under $100</h2>
<h3>Home + Cleaning Services</h3>
<h2>1. House Cleaning</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $50 for supplies. Charge $40 to $80 per home</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Apartments, WhatsApp groups, "first clean 50% off"</p>
<h2>2. Window Washing</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $40 squeegee + spray. Charge $10 per window</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Shops, offices, homes</p>
<h2>3. Laundry Pickup</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $0. Use your time. Charge $15 per load</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Students, busy professionals</p>
<h2>4. Organizing + Decluttering</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $0. Charge $25/hour</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Parents, people moving</p>
<h3>Outdoor + Repair Services</h3>
<h2>5. Lawn Care</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $80 used mower. Charge $30 per yard</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Door knock in 1 neighborhood</p>
<h2>6. Pressure Washing</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $90 rent washer first job. Charge $150 per driveway</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Before/after photos on Instagram</p>
<h2>7. Handyman - Small Jobs</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $100 tool kit. Charge $40/hour</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: "I hang shelves, fix leaks, assemble furniture"</p>
<h2>8. Junk Removal</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $0. Use truck + charge $80 per load</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Post "Free pickup" then charge to dump</p>
<h3>Pet + People Services</h3>
<h2>9. Dog Walking</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $20 leashes. Charge $20 per 30 min walk</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Flyers at vet, Rover app</p>
<h2>10. Pet Sitting</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $0. Charge $30 per day</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Referrals + Facebook groups</p>
<h2>11. Tutoring</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $0. Charge $25 to $50/hour</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Parents, school boards</p>
<h2>12. Personal Chef / Meal Prep</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $80 groceries. Charge $100 for 10 meals</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Busy families, gym people</p>
<h3>Business + Tech Services</h3>
<h2>13. Social Media Management</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $0. Charge $300/month per business</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Local shops with bad Instagram</p>
<h2>14. Flyer + Poster Design</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $0 Canva. Charge $50 per design</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Events, shops, restaurants</p>
<h2>15. Phone/Computer Setup</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $0. Charge $40 to fix or setup</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Seniors, small businesses</p>
<h2>16. Resume Writing</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $0. Charge $60 per resume</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: LinkedIn, students</p>
<h3>Specialty + Niche Services</h3>
<h2>17. Mobile Car Wash</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $100 bucket + supplies. Charge $40 per car</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Offices, apartments</p>
<h2>18. Event Decor</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $100 balloons. Charge $300 per party</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Birthdays, weddings on Instagram</p>
<h2>19. Photography - Portraits</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $0 phone. Charge $100 for 1 hour shoot</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Families, small business owners</p>
<h2>20. Errand Runner</h2>
<p><strong>Start with</strong>: $0. Charge $25/hour + costs</p>
<p><strong>Get clients</strong>: Busy professionals, elderly</p>
<h2>The 7-Day "First $500" Plan</h2>
<p><strong>Day 1-2</strong>: Pick 1 service. Buy supplies. Make simple flyer on Canva.</p>
<p><strong>Day 3-4</strong>: Offer 3 people "50% off first job for testimonial".</p>
<p><strong>Day 5-7</strong>: Deliver amazing work. Ask for 5 Google reviews + referrals.</p>
<p>Goal: 5 customers x $100 avg = $500</p>
<h2>How To Get Customers For $0</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>WhatsApp Status + Groups</strong>: "I now offer"</li>
<li><strong>Google Business Profile</strong>: Free. Ranks you locally</li>
<li><strong>Door knocking</strong>: 20 doors = 2 customers</li>
<li><strong>Referrals</strong>: "Refer a friend, both get 20% off"</li>
</ol>
<h2>Common Mistakes That Kill You At $0</h2>
<p>1. <strong>Too many services</strong>: Pick 1. Master it</p>
<p>2. <strong>Cheap prices</strong>: $15 cleaning attracts bad clients. Charge $40+</p>
<p>3. <strong>No follow up</strong>: "Book next month and save 15%"</p>
<p>4. <strong>Waiting for perfect</strong>: Start messy. Improve with first 10 clients</p>
<h2>FAQ: $100 Businesses</h2>
<p><strong>1. Do I need a license?</strong></p>
<p>Start first. Register after you make $1000. Check local rules in Bishkek.</p>
<p><strong>2. What if I have no skills?</strong></p>
<p>Pick "errand runner" or "organizing". You learn on job 1.</p>
<p><strong>3. How fast to $1000/month?</strong></p>
<p>Month 1: $500. Month 2: $1000 with 10 recurring clients.</p>
<p>People do not pay for "cleaning". They pay for "clean house without doing it".</p>
<p>Pick 1 problem. Charge to solve it. Show up on time.</p>
<p>With $100 and 7 days you can have a business that pays you cash.</p>
