<p>The Greeks did not break down the walls of Troy. </p>
<p>They hid inside a gift. The Trojans brought it in themselves. Then the Greeks won.</p>
<p>Most local businesses try to "break down the wall" with cold calls, ads, and "hire me" DMs.</p>
<p>That is why no one answers.</p>
<p>The Trojan Horse Strategy: <strong>Give a gift so valuable they invite you in. Then deliver your service.</strong></p>
<p>This is how you get your first 10 local clients in 30 days without ads or experience.</p>
<h2>Why Cold Outreach Fails in 2027</h2>
<p>"Hey I do social media. Want to work together?"</p>
<p>Business owners get 20 of those per week. The wall is up.</p>
<p><strong>The Trojan Horse Rule</strong>: Do not sell the horse. Sell the gift inside the horse.</p>
<p>People do not buy services. They buy results they can see for free first.</p>
<h2>The 5-Step Trojan Horse Strategy</h2>
<h2>Step 1: Build The Horse - Pick Your Free Gift</h2>
<p>Your gift must do 3 things: Be fast, be visual, be specific.</p>
<p><strong>Bad Horse</strong>: "Free consultation"</p>
<p><strong>Good Horse</strong>: "Free 3-post Instagram audit with mockups"</p>

<p>Rule: It must take you less than 2 hours to deliver.</p>
<h2>Step 2: Roll The Horse To The Gates - Find 50 Targets</h2>
<p>Do not DM "big" businesses. They already have agencies.</p>
<p><strong>Target</strong>: Businesses with bad Google photos, no posts in 30 days, or 10 to 50 reviews.</p>
<p><strong>Where to find them</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Google Maps</strong>: Search "dentist Bishkek" and look at photos</li>
<li><strong>Instagram</strong>: Local businesses with less than 1000 followers</li>
<li><strong>Walk in</strong>: 10 businesses on one street. Bring the gift printed</li>
</ul>
<p>Goal: 50 names. Not 500.</p>
<h2>Step 3: The Trojans Pull It In - The Message</h2>
<p>Do not pitch. Give the gift.</p>
<p><strong>DM / Email / In-Person Script</strong></p>
<p>"Hey, I noticed your. I made you a free. No pitch. If you like it, use it. If you want 10 more like it, I can do that for you."</p>
<p><strong>Why it works</strong>: You gave first. You showed proof. You removed risk.</p>
<p>30% to 50% will say "thank you". 10% to 20% will ask "how much".</p>
<h2>Step 4: The Greeks Come Out At Night - The Delivery</h2>
<p>Overdeliver on the free gift. This is where you win.</p>
<p><strong>Spartan Rules for Delivery</strong></p>
<p>1. <strong>Deliver in 48 hours</strong>: Speed shocks them</p>
<p>2. <strong>Make it visual</strong>: Mockup, video, PDF. Not just words</p>
<p>3. <strong>Add 1 extra</strong>: "I also made you a logo version"</p>
<p>4. <strong>Ask 1 question</strong>: "Want me to do this for all 20 products?"</p>
<h2>Step 5: Open The Gates - Convert To Paid</h2>
<p>After they love the gift, offer the full version.</p>
<p><strong>Conversion Script</strong></p>
<p>"Glad you liked the 3 posts. I do 12 per month for $400. Includes captions, hashtags, and scheduling. Want me to start next Monday?"</p>
<p><strong>The Trojan Horse Rule</strong>: The paid offer is just "more of the free gift".</p>
<h2>Your 30-Day Plan To Get 10 Clients</h2>
<p><strong>Week 1: Build + Target</strong></p>
<p>Pick 1 gift. Find 50 local businesses. Make a list with name, owner, and phone.</p>
<p><strong>Week 2: Deliver 20 Horses</strong></p>
<p>Send 10 DMs and visit 10 businesses. Deliver all 20 gifts.</p>
<p><strong>Week 3: Deliver 20 More + Follow Up</strong></p>
<p>Follow up with Week 2. "Did you use the mockup?" Pitch paid.</p>
<p><strong>Week 4: Close + Referrals</strong></p>
<p>Close 5 to 10 clients. Ask each: "Who else in town needs this?"</p>
<p>Goal: 10 paying clients at $300 to $800/month = $3000 to $8000/month.</p>
<h2>Common Ways The Trojan Horse Fails</h2>
<p>1. <strong>Gift is too big</strong>: If it takes 10 hours, you will quit. Keep it to 2 hours</p>
<p>2. <strong>Pitching too early</strong>: Give first. Wait for them to ask</p>
<p>3. <strong>Generic gift</strong>: "Free audit" is weak. "Free 3 Instagram posts" is strong</p>
<p>4. <strong>No follow up</strong>: 80% of yes comes on follow up #2</p>
<h2>Tools Greeks Would Use in 2027</h2>
<p>1. <strong>Build Horse</strong>: Canva for mockups, CapCut for videos, ChatGPT for copy</p>
<p>2. <strong>Find Gates</strong>: Google Maps, Instagram, Walk</p>
<p>3. <strong>Deliver</strong>: Google Drive, Loom to record walkthrough</p>
<p>4. <strong>Collect Payment</strong>: Stripe, Payoneer, Cash</p>
<h2>FAQ: Trojan Horse Strategy</h2>
<p><strong>1. What if they take the free gift and ghost?</strong></p>
<p>Some will. That is why you send 50. 10 will pay. That is 20% conversion.</p>
<p><strong>2. Do I need a portfolio?</strong></p>
<p>No. The free gift IS your portfolio.</p>
<p><strong>3. Can this work for any service?</strong></p>
<p>Yes. If you can show the result before they pay, you can use a Trojan Horse.</p>
<p>Greeks wasted 10 years trying to break Troy’s walls.</p>
<p>They won in 1 night with a gift.</p>
<p>Stop selling. Start gifting.</p>
<p>Make something so good they have to let you in. Then prove you deserve to stay.</p>
<p>Do this for 30 days and you will have 10 local clients, testimonials, and cash flow.</p>
