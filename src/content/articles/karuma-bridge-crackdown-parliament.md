<h2><span>Members of Parliament are demanding answers from the government regarding the ramifications of restricted access to the Karuma Bridge, a vital link between North and West Nile regions with the rest of Uganda. This comes after the bridge, crucial for trade and travel, was found compromised, with cracks rendering it unsafe for heavy vehicles.</span></h2><div><a href="https://images.getimg.ai/resize?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimg.getimg.ai%2Fgenerated%2Fimg-lUMa0kJsdKinXlJDZbHKD.jpeg&amp;type=auto&amp;width=1080&amp;speed=5"></a></div><br><p><br></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>In response to Speaker Anita Among's communication, MPs expressed concerns about the significant impact the traffic restrictions would have on trade, livelihoods, and various other aspects of daily life. They are calling on the Minister for Works and Transport to elucidate the situation.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>Speaker Among, presiding over the plenary sitting on Monday, May 6, 2024, urged the Uganda National Roads Authority to expedite construction works on the bridge. MPs highlighted the limitations of alternative routes, such as the one through Murchison National Park, which not only adds to travel time but also imposes additional costs, hindering business operations.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>Dickson Kateshumbwa, representing Sheema Municipality, emphasized the increased costs of doing business, especially for cross-border traders. Similarly, George Bhoka from Obongi County raised concerns about the doubling of bus fares, making travel unaffordable for many in the West Nile region.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>Jesca Ababiku, the Adjumani District Woman MP, shared her firsthand experience of delays at the bridge and stressed the need for the Works Ministry to provide users with essential information about the situation. Meanwhile, Erute County South MP, Hon. Jonathan Odur, called for additional ferries and increased navigation frequency to alleviate the strain on alternative routes.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>MPs lamented that Karuma Bridge is just one of many in need of urgent repair, given the looming threat of floods and landslides.
