Reading Regularly</span></h3><p dir="ltr"><span face="Arial, sans-serif">Reading regularly is one of the most effective ways to enhance your intelligence. Practicing Mindfulness and Meditation</span></h3><p dir="ltr"><span face="Arial, sans-serif">Mindfulness and meditation are powerful tools for enhancing cognitive function and mental clarity.
