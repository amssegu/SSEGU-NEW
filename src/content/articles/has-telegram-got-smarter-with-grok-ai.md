<p> For the Telegram enthusiasts, be it for media sharing like movies, its security features, or simply as a messaging platform—well, it has got even better. Telegram has recently integrated with Grok AI, Elon Musk’s AI that has been quite the popular kid on his X platform, now, available on Telegram as well!</p><p>If you’ve been keeping an eye on AI chatbots, you might have already heard of Grok AI. Developed by xAI, Elon Musk’s artificial intelligence venture, Grok AI was initially available exclusively on X (formerly Twitter). However, in a stunt to expand its reach, Grok AI has now made its way onto Telegram, providing users with an alternative way to engage with its witty, informative, and sometimes unfiltered responses.</p><h5>When Did Grok AI Integrate with Telegram?</h5><p>The integration has been spotted just recently (late March 2025) and has appeared in headlines promoting both Telegram and Grok AI. As Telegram continues to evolve beyond just a messaging platform, this addition strengthens its appeal, particularly among users who are keen on AI-driven interactions.</p><h5>Who Can Access Grok AI on Telegram?</h5><p>Before you rush to test it out, there’s a catch—Grok AI is currently not available to just anyone--as of now, access is restricted to <strong>Telegram Premium subscribers and X Premium users</strong>. If you’re subscribed to either service, you can interact with Grok AI directly by searching for "@GrokAI" in Telegram and starting a chat.</p><p>There’s no official word yet on whether Grok AI will be available for free-tier users in the future.</p><h5>What Makes Grok AI Different?</h5><p>If you have been using chatbots for your varied reasons, you have definitely noticed their trend of speech. well, unlike conventional AI chatbots that stick to a neutral and polished tone, Grok AI prides itself on being more <strong>humorous, insightful, and sometimes a little edgy</strong>. Inspired by "The Hitchhiker’s Guide to the Galaxy," it brings an unconventional approach to AI conversations. With the latest <strong>Grok 3 model</strong>, the chatbot is reportedly ten times more capable than its predecessors, offering more advanced reasoning, research capabilities, and creative content generation.</p><p>as for how this affects the public,</p><p>Telegram users can now interact with a powerful AI assistant within their favorite messaging app, a kind of ai-enhanced conversation.</p><p>Grok AI isn’t just for answering questions—it can assist with content creation, brainstorming ideas, and even engaging in casual banter, which positions it as "more than just a chatbot"</p><p>With AI tools becoming an integral part of digital communication, this integration puts Telegram in direct competition with other platforms incorporating AI-driven features.</p><h5>Not Everyone Is Happy</h5><p>While the Grok AI integration is exciting, it has also stirred some controversy. The chatbot has faced scrutiny over its <strong>unfiltered responses</strong>, leading to regulatory concerns. In fact, India’s Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology has reportedly sought clarification from X regarding some of Grok’s more questionable replies. This raises questions about content moderation and how AI chatbots should be regulated on messaging platforms.</p><p>Still, many find the update a much-welcomed feature and are eagerly interacting with it. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast, a chatbot aficionado, or just someone who enjoys casual AI banter, this is an update worth exploring.</p><p>So, if you’re a Telegram Premium subscriber, give it a try and see what all the buzz is about.
