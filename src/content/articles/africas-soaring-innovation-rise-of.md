unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) gain increasing importance for modern armed forces, African countries are investing in local drone manufacturing to gain a strategic edge.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>According to the latest data from Military Africa, up to 35 different made-in-Africa drone models are either operational or beyond the prototype phase, showcasing significant progress and innovation across the continent.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>These UAVs, developed across seven African countries by thirteen different companies, represent Africa's growing expertise in drone technology.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>Countries like Egypt, Sudan, South Africa, Nigeria, Kenya, and Ethiopia are leading the charge in indigenous drone production, with notable contributions from both governmental and private entities.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>Currently, there are approximately 180 locally-made technology.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>South Africa's Denel stands out as Africa's foremost drone manufacturer, tracing its roots back to the 1970s when the military initiated drone development, leading to a thriving industry by the 1980s.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>In a significant milestone, South Africa's Milkor 380, boasting impressive specifications, marked its inaugural flight in September 2023, underlining the nation's prowess in UAV development.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>Nigeria's Airforce Institute of Technology (AFIT) emerges as Africa's second-largest drone manufacturer, with notable contributions like the Tsaigumi UAV, a product of collaboration with international partners.</span></p><p><span><br></span></p><p><span>North African countries like Egypt are not far behind, with a mix of locally-built and imported drones enhancing their defense capabilities.
