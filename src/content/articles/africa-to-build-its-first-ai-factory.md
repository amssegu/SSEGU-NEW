<p> Africa has always been considered a step behind when it comes to technology. And I mean, only just recently, we started embracing STEM initiatives—years after other continents had already made tech a core part of their everyday lives. Notably, we haven't had any big AI tech truly take root in Africa, which is why when Cassava Technologies announced plans to build an AI factory, I was beyond thrilled. I’m a tech enthusiast, after all!</p><p>The initiative comes after Cassava Technologies, founded by Zimbabwean entrepreneur <strong>Strive Masiyiwa</strong>, has joined forces with NVIDIA to bring cutting-edge AI infrastructure to the continent. This means that for the first time, African businesses, researchers, and innovators will have direct access to AI supercomputing power without relying on external resources.</p><h3><strong>What Does an <a href="https://info.ssegu.com/?s=artificial+intelligence">AI</a> Factory Do, Anyway?</strong></h3><p>If you're wondering what exactly an AI factory is, think of it as a powerhouse that fuels machine learning, deep learning, and artificial intelligence projects. It provides the computing power needed to train AI models, process massive datasets, and develop advanced AI applications...to put it plainly, an AI factory provides the tools and power required to make Artificial Intelligence.</p><p>With NVIDIA’s <strong>supercomputers and AI software</strong>, Cassava’s AI factory will enable African industries to build their own AI solutions.</p><p>We’ve all seen how AI is transforming industries globally, but Africa has often been left playing catch-up due to <strong>limited infrastructure, funding, and expertise</strong>. This AI factory changes that.</p><ul><li><strong>Boosting Local Innovation:</strong> African AI developers will now have a <strong>homegrown resource</strong> to build and test their AI models without relying on overseas data centers.</li><li><strong>Economic Growth:</strong> AI-driven industries create jobs, stimulate investments, and encourage the growth of <strong>African tech startups</strong>.</li><li><strong>Problem-Solving at Scale:</strong> Imagine AI-driven solutions tackling issues like <strong>climate change, food security, and medical diagnostics</strong>—problems uniquely affecting Africa.</li></ul><p>The AI factory will first be set up in <strong>South Africa</strong>, with future expansions planned for <strong>Egypt, Kenya, Morocco, and Nigeria</strong>. Cassava Technologies is also integrating this with its pan-African <strong>fiber-optic network</strong>, ensuring high-speed connectivity for AI services with the sole aim of making <strong>AI as a Service (AIaaS)</strong> a reality for African businesses and researchers. - <a href="https://www.cassavatechnologies.com/cassava-to-upgrade-its-data-centres-with-nvidia-supercomputers-to-drive-africas-ai-future/">Cassava to Upgrade its Data Centres with NVIDIA SuperComputers</a></p><p>And let’s not forget the sustainability angle—these AI supercomputers will run on <strong>energy-efficient infrastructure</strong>, minimizing electricity consumption while delivering <strong>maximum computational power</strong>.</p><h3><strong>How The Tech Industry Reacts</strong></h3><p>Tech experts are already calling this a <strong>defining moment for Africa’s AI ecosystem, </strong>I am not an AI developer and even I find this thrilling. NVIDIA’s <strong>Jaap Zuiderveld</strong>, VP for EMEA, highlighted how AI is transforming industries worldwide, and Africa shouldn’t be left behind. Cassava’s <strong>Strive Masiyiwa</strong> also emphasized that this project will enable Africans to develop AI-driven solutions <strong>without looking outside the continent</strong> for resources. And while there’s no denying that this is a <strong>bold and ambitious</strong> move, the reality of whether Africa is ready to take it on is another issue entirely.</p><p>That said, I’d argue that we’ve always been ready—we’ve just lacked the necessary infrastructure.</p><p>The question now isn’t whether Africa can build AI—it’s how fast we can scale it up. And personally? I can’t wait to see what’s coming next.</p><p><a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-03-24/nvidia-tapped-by-strive-masiyiwa-to-build-first-ai-factory-in-africa">Nvidia Tapped by Tycoon Masiyiwa to Build First AI Factory in Africa</a></p><div>Content originally published on SSEGU.
