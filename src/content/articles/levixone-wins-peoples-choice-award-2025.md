<p> Ugandan gospel musician Levixone has been honored with the People’s Choice Award at the East African Gospel Music Awards (EAGMA) 2025, held in Tanzania.</p><p>“Glory glory to God allow me congratulate my country Uganda for being recognized in the @eagmaafricaAWARDS Tanzania 🇹🇿 2025 we won People’s choice award in East Africa . Thank you everyone for voting 🗳️” Levixone posted expressing his gratitude in winning the award for his Country Uganda.</p><blockquote><p dir="ltr" lang="en">Glory glory to God allow me congratulate my country Uganda for being recognized in the <a href="https://twitter.com/eagmaafrica?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw">@eagmaafrica</a> AWARDS Tanzania 🇹🇿 2025 we won People’s choice award in East Africa . Thank you everyone for voting 🗳️ <a href="https://twitter.com/hashtag/WeWinTogether?src=hash&amp;ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw">#WeWinTogether</a> 🙏 <a href="https://t.co/rMHdqeQCXd">pic.twitter.com/rMHdqeQCXd</a></p><p>— Levixone (@levixone) <a href="https://twitter.com/levixone/status/1939276727630610677?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw">June 29, 2025</a></p></blockquote><p></p><p>This prestigious ceremony celebrates and recognizes gospel artists from across East Africa, providing a vital platform for them to showcase their talent and gain regional Recognition .</p><p>Levixone, Multi award winner,singer and song writer, has had a remarkable journey in the music industry.</p><p>His passion for music ignited at a young age, as he began singing in church at the age of 10. This early exposure laid the foundation for a professional career that officially started in 2010.</p><p>Since then, Levixone has become a prominent figure in the gospel music scene in Uganda and globally, known for his soulful R&amp;B, Soul, and Reggae-infused gospel tracks.</p><p>The EAGMA 2025 event underscores the cultural significance of gospel music in East Africa, bringing together artists from various countries to celebrate their contributions.</p><p>Levixone’s win in the People’s Choice category, where the public votes for their favorite artist, highlights his widespread popularity and the impact of his music on audiences across the region and his Country.</p><p>The win is part of a broader  success in Levixone’s music journey.
